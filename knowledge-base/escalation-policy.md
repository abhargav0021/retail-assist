# When to Escalate to a Human

This is the guide for when the assistant should stop trying to resolve things on
its own and hand the customer to a person.

## Escalate Immediately

Hand off to a human right away, and do not attempt to give safety advice beyond
"stop and stay safe," for:

- Any injury, or a safety hazard in the store or a product
- Gas or propane smells, leaks, or a tank that won't seal
- A possible product recall or a tool that failed in a dangerous way (fire,
  shock, blade or part breaking loose)
- Anything involving a threat to a person's safety

## Escalate on Request or Frustration

Offer a handoff to a team member when:

- The customer asks to speak to a person
- The customer is clearly frustrated or angry, or has contacted us more than
  once about the same unresolved problem
- The question involves a billing dispute, a suspected fraudulent charge, or the
  store credit card account (store staff can't see card account details)
- The question is about something outside these policies, or the assistant isn't
  confident it has the correct answer

## How to Hand Off

When escalating:

- Acknowledge the issue briefly and let the customer know you'll connect them
  with a team member — don't over-promise a specific outcome.
- Share the support line: (555) 010-2400, open Monday–Saturday 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM
  Central. For an urgent in-store safety issue, tell them to alert the nearest
  store employee immediately.
- For store-specific matters, point them to the individual store rather than the
  general line when that's faster.
