# Products & In-Store Services

## Tool Rental

Most stores rent tools from the Rental Desk near the Pro Desk. Deep Ellum does
not have a Rental Desk.

- Rates run about $35–$75 per day depending on the tool, with a discounted
  weekly rate (roughly 4x the daily rate for a full week).
- A refundable deposit is held on a credit card. The deposit amount depends on
  the tool's replacement value.
- You need a valid government photo ID to rent. The renter must be 18 or older.
- Common rentals: carpet cleaners, tile saws, augers, floor sanders, pressure
  washers, and demolition hammers.
- Late returns are billed at the daily rate per day. If a tool comes back
  damaged, repair costs come out of the deposit.

## Paint Mixing & Color Matching

Every store has a paint desk.

- We mix interior and exterior paint in most major brands we carry.
- Color matching from a sample (a chip, a piece of trim, or a photo printed on
  paper) is free. Matching from a phone screen is not reliable, so bring a
  physical sample when you can.
- Custom-mixed paint is non-returnable once it's tinted.
- Typical wait is 10–15 minutes when the desk is busy on weekends.

## Key Cutting

Standard house and padlock keys can be cut at the hardware counter, usually
while you wait. We do not cut high-security, transponder, or car keys. Cut keys
are non-returnable.

## Propane Exchange & Refill

- Propane tank exchange is available at all stores at the outdoor pickup area.
- We refill 20 lb grill tanks at most stores; refill is not available at Deep
  Ellum or Preston Hollow.
- We do not fill tanks that are expired, rusted, or out of certification date.
  Exchange is the option in that case.

## Product Categories

We carry lumber and building materials, plumbing, electrical, hardware and
fasteners, power and hand tools, paint, lawn and garden, seasonal, and cleaning
supplies. The Deep Ellum store is smaller and does not carry a full lumber yard
or large appliances — it focuses on tools, hardware, paint, and small
project supplies.
