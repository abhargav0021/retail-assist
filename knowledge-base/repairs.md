# Power Tool Repairs

## What We Service

We take in most corded and cordless power tools, plus outdoor power equipment
like mowers, trimmers, and blowers, for repair. We do not service tools we don't
carry, and we can't repair anything with obvious unsafe modifications.

## Repair Intake Process

- Drop the tool off at the Pro Desk at any store. Deep Ellum can take drop-offs
  but sends tools to the Camp Bowie store for service, which adds a few days.
- There is a diagnostic fee (usually around $30) to assess the tool. If you go
  ahead with the paid repair, the diagnostic fee is applied toward the repair
  cost.
- Typical turnaround is 7–14 business days. Parts on order can extend that; the
  service team will call with an estimate before doing any paid work.
- Bring your receipt if the tool might still be under manufacturer warranty — it
  changes who pays.

## Warranty vs. Paid Repairs

If the tool is within the manufacturer's warranty and the failure is covered,
the repair is handled as a warranty claim and you don't pay for covered parts or
labor. If it's out of warranty, or the damage isn't covered (for example, wear
items or user damage), it's a paid repair and we'll quote it before starting.

## Loaner Tools

Loaner tools are available to Pro account members while their tool is in for a
warranty repair, subject to availability. Loaners are not offered for
paid/out-of-warranty repairs.
