# Returns & Warranties

## Return Window

- With a receipt: most items can be returned within 90 days for a refund to the
  original payment method.
- Without a receipt: we can often look up purchases made on a credit or debit
  card or a Cedar & Bolt Pro account. If we can find the purchase, normal return
  rules apply. If we can't, returns within 90 days are eligible for store credit
  at the item's current selling price, with a valid photo ID.
- Pro account holders get an extended 365-day return window on most items.

## Non-Returnable Items

Some items can't be returned once sold:

- Custom-mixed (tinted) paint
- Cut lumber, cut pipe, cut blinds, and other cut-to-order materials
- Cut keys
- Clearance and "as-is" floor models marked final sale
- Gift cards

Special-order items may be subject to a restocking fee — the Pro Desk will note
this at the time of the order.

## Manufacturer Warranties

Many power tools, water heaters, and outdoor power equipment carry a
manufacturer warranty that lasts longer than our 90-day return window. After 90
days, warranty claims go through the manufacturer, not the store. Keep your
receipt — most manufacturers require proof of purchase. Store staff can help you
find the manufacturer's warranty contact info.

## Defective Items

If an item is defective out of the box, bring it back with the receipt within
the return window and we'll exchange or refund it. For items that fail after the
return window but within a manufacturer warranty, we'll help you start a
warranty claim or, for power tools, take it in for repair (see the repairs
guide).
