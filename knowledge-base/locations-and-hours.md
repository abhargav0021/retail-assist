# Store Locations & Hours

## Standard Hours

Most Cedar & Bolt stores keep the same hours:

- Monday–Friday: 6:30 AM – 9:00 PM
- Saturday: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Sunday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM

A few locations open earlier for contractors (see the store list below). The
Pro Desk generally opens 30 minutes before the main store on weekdays.

Hours can change around bad weather. If a store closes early for weather, we
post it on the store's voicemail greeting and the website banner.

## Holiday Closures

All stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On Christmas Eve
and New Year's Eve, stores close at 5:00 PM. On July 4th, Memorial Day, and
Labor Day, stores keep normal weekend hours unless posted otherwise. The Pro
Desk is closed on all major holidays even when the main store is open.
