# Contractor Pro Desk

## Pro Account Benefits

The Pro Desk serves contractors, trades, and volume buyers. Pro account
benefits include:

- Volume and bulk pricing on qualifying orders
- A dedicated checkout line at the Pro Desk
- An extended 365-day return window on most items
- Purchase tracking and reprintable receipts tied to the account
- Loaner tools during warranty repairs (subject to availability)

## Bulk & Special Orders

- We can special-order items we don't stock or larger quantities than are on the
  shelf. Lead times are typically 3–10 business days depending on the supplier.
- Special orders may require a deposit and can carry a restocking fee if
  cancelled after the supplier ships. The Pro Desk notes this on the order.
- For large lumber and material orders, ask about will-call staging so the whole
  order is pulled and ready at once.

## Job-Site Delivery

Stores with a lumber yard offer job-site delivery for large orders. Delivery
windows and fees depend on distance and load size, and are quoted when the order
is placed. Someone must be on site to receive and sign for the delivery, or you
can arrange a drop location in advance.

## How to Enroll

Enroll at the Pro Desk at any store. Bring a business name and contact info; a
reseller or tax-exemption certificate is optional and only needed if you want
tax-exempt purchasing. There's no fee to open a Pro account.
