# Orders, Pickup & Financing

## Order Status

You can check an online order with your order number and the email used at
checkout — no account needed. Order confirmation and "ready for pickup" updates
are sent by email. If an order looks stuck for more than 24 hours after you
expected it, contact the store on the order or call support at (555) 010-2400.

## In-Store & Curbside Pickup

- Most in-stock online orders are ready for pickup within 2 hours during store
  hours. You'll get an email when it's ready — please wait for that email before
  coming in.
- Bring a photo ID and your order number. If someone else is picking up, add
  them as an alternate pickup person on the order, and they'll need their own ID.
- Curbside pickup is available at all stores except Deep Ellum. Park in a marked
  curbside spot and call the number on the sign.
- We hold pickup orders for 5 days. After that, unclaimed orders are cancelled
  and refunded to the original payment method.

## Delivery

Local delivery is available on large items and lumber from stores with a lumber
yard. Delivery fees and windows depend on distance and load size; the store
quotes this at checkout. The Deep Ellum store does not offer lumber delivery.

## Payment & Financing

- We accept major credit and debit cards, cash, and the Cedar & Bolt store card.
- The Cedar & Bolt store card is issued by a third-party bank; applications and
  balances are handled by the bank, not the store.
- Promotional financing is available on qualifying purchases over $299 with the
  store card. Terms vary by promotion and are set by the bank. Store staff can't
  see or change your financing account details — the bank's customer service
  number is on the back of the card.
